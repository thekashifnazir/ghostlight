## 17 · pack · Task 7 done (coverage checker)

For the covered chapters, check room presence, hotspot presence (set of census nums per room), door destinations (to equals census toRoom unless a note overrides), stale entries, and exit-graph agreement; treat doorGates flags as real; collect every gap and fail if any room/hotspot/door is uncovered.
What Kiro did: wrote `packs/tools/src/coverage.ts`. Typecheck clean. Smoke test on the draft pack for chapters 1 and 2: zero gaps. Negative test (remove a hotspot, break a door destination): three gaps reported (missing-hotspot, door-destination, exit-graph), confirming it is not trivially passing.
Files changed: `.kiro/specs/pack/tasks.md`, `packs/tools/src/coverage.ts`, `docs/kiro-process.md`.
Time: about 30 minutes.
What went wrong or surprised you: the census shows room 62 has three doors (104, 105, 106) all with toRoom 63, but the draft pack puts 104 under objects (street door switched off in chapter 1) and 105 as to:null (curtained doorway that runs the bedtime cutscene). What was learned: the real door quirks in room 62 are exactly the override cases the design anticipated; grounding the checker against the actual census early caught that the exits-only rule would have failed a correct pack.
